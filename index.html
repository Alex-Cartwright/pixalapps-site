PIXAL

A little creature shares a cabin with you. He lives on your clock, your weather and the music you’re playing — and gets on with his day whether or not you’re watching.

Coming to iPhone

01 · A world that keeps its own time

Dawn, day, dusk and night are different places to be.

The sky above this page is the game’s own: nine flat colours in dithered bands, swapped for the hour and the season. Pixal reads the real clock and the real weather, so a rainy Tuesday evening looks like one.

Lamps and the hearth are drawn as stacks of hard-edged discs, seven bands each, stepping along the pixel grid. No soft gradients anywhere. The banding is the point.

02 · Cooking together

Prep

You cook from what’s actually in the larder. Three ingredients, one pan, and a creature who has opinions about all of it.

Toss

The first time he makes something new, the kitchen becomes a little scene — a bold frame, a push in, a held pose before the hit.

Sizzle

Steam, sparks and a worried face. Dishes aren’t a list handed to you: a recipe gets its name only once he recognises it.

Reveal

Then the payoff, and a page in the recipe book he writes himself. His palate becomes a taste map you can actually look at.

03 · He works out what he likes

Every time he settles somewhere, the moment is scored.

♪

He hears your music

Put something on in any app and he notices within seconds. Whether he joins in is up to him — his mood, the hour and what he’s busy with decide it. He’s a pet, not a mirror.

♥

Favourite places

Was the fire lit? A record on? Rain at the glass? Each moment becomes a fondness for that chair or that desk, fading over a fortnight unless something keeps it alive.

✿

A garden through the year

Beds by the front door, crops that only take in their own season, and a frost that will claim whatever’s still in the ground.

➶

Days out

He takes himself off along named routes and comes home with what he found — and sometimes, instead of a thing, a whole new place.
