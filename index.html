PIXAL

This is Pixal, your pixel pal. He shares a cabin with you, lives on your clock, your weather and the music you’re playing — and gets on with his day whether or not you’re watching.

Coming to iPhone

01 · Meet Pixal

Your pixel pal, growing up alongside you.

Pixal is a small creature who lives in your phone and keeps you company. He has moods, a taste in food and music, and a favourite chair he chose himself. He cooks with you, tends the garden, reads, wanders off for the day and comes home with stories.

He isn’t waiting for instructions. He gets on with his day whether or not you’re watching — and over weeks his hobbies level up, his opinions settle in, and the cabin starts to look like someone lives there.

02 · Make it home

Start with an empty cabin. Make it his.

  1. The back garden A lemon tree, a koi pond, a deckchair. He’ll sit and watch the fish for ages.
  2. The kitchen A stove, a kettle, a larder of what you actually bought — and him at the hob.
  3. The bedroom A paper lantern, a bonsai, his desk by the window. He writes there before bed.
  4. The lounge The hearth, a sofa, a shelf of records. Headphones on, he’s not to be disturbed.
  5. The front garden A red lantern, a sleeping cat, and him at the gate to say hello.

Every piece here is drawn by the game itself — sets from a Japanese lounge to mid-century modern, bought with coins he earns and placed wherever you like.

03 · Things you do together

Cook, grow, listen, write, wander.

  1. prep
  2. toss
  3. sizzle
  4. reveal

Cook

Three ingredients from your real larder, one pan, and a creature with opinions about all of it. The first time he makes something new, the kitchen becomes a little cinematic — and a recipe gets its name only once he recognises it.

Grow

Beds by the back door and crops that only take in their own season — peas in spring, pumpkins in autumn, kale through the winter. A ripe bed waits for you; a frost won’t.

Listen

Put music on in any app and he notices within seconds. Whether he joins in is up to him: his mood, the hour and what he’s busy with decide it. He’s a pet, not a mirror.

Write & read

Books level up his reading, and the reading unlocks whole furniture sets. At his desk he keeps a recipe book and a journal of the days you’ve had.

Days out

He takes himself off along named routes to shops, a fishing pond and places further out — and sometimes, instead of a thing, he comes home with a whole new place.

04 · He works out what he likes

Every time he settles somewhere, the moment is scored.

♥

Favourite places

Was the fire lit? A record on? Rain at the glass? Each moment becomes a fondness for that chair or that desk, fading over a fortnight unless something keeps it alive.
