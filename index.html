Pixal

Our first app — in development

Pixal is a quiet game about keeping someone company. You share a cabin with a small creature. There is nothing to win and nothing to lose: it potters about, forms opinions, and gets on with its day whether or not you are watching.

A world that keeps its own time

The world runs on real time. Dawn, day, dusk and night each light the room differently; spring, summer, autumn and winter change what grows and what you'll find outside; and the weather rolls through clear, cloud, rain, wind, fog and snow. None of it is a backdrop image — the sky, the landscape and the trail are generated as you go, keyed to the same palette, and they never repeat.

Things to do together

  • Cook, and keep a larder. Gather what's in season, learn recipes, and put meals together in a working kitchen.
  • Keep a garden. Beds by the front door, sown and tended through the seasons.
  • Go on outings. Fishing, foraging and runs along named routes, with a field book of finds that only appear in the right weather.
  • Furnish the place. Buy, move and arrange furniture — and your creature quietly works out which chair it likes best, and why.
  • Put a record on. It responds to music you're actually playing.
  • Walk it off. A micropet hatches from your real step count.

How it's made

Pixal is built with React Native and Expo, with the game itself drawn through Skia — a custom pixel-art renderer written for this app rather than an off-the-shelf game engine. The world, the lighting and the creature's behaviour are all simulated in code we wrote and test ourselves.
